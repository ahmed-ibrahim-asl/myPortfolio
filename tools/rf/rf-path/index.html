Understand the system

Radiated power and spreading

EIRP is transmitter power times antenna gain toward the receiver, after feeder losses. PFD is watts per square metre at a specified range. Doubling range spreads the same energy over four times the area, reducing PFD by 6.02 dB.

EIRP=PtGt/Lt,S=EIRP4πd2EIRP=P_tG_t/L_t,\quad S=\frac{EIRP}{4\pi d^2}EIRP=Pt​Gt​/Lt​,S=4πd2EIRP​

P_t: W; G_t: linear gain; L_t ≥ 1: linear feeder loss; d: m; S: W/m².

EIRP is directional equivalent power, not total physical RF output.

Source: week 3/reference.pdf · PDF page 74

Common mistakes

  • 92.44 requires km and GHz; 32.44 requires km and MHz.
  • PFD and received power have different units.
  • Do not include antenna gain twice after using EIRP.

Worked approach

Inverse square law

FSPL describes geometric spreading between ideal isotropic antenna references. At 10 km and 6 GHz it is approximately 128.0 dB. For d in km and f in GHz, use the 92.44 convenience constant; the fundamental equation uses consistent SI units.
