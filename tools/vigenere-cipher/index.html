Text & Encoding / letter transformations

Vigenère & Autokey Cipher

Encode and decode with a repeating Vigenère key or a self-extending Autokey stream, and watch the key stream build letter by letter.

Answer first

How do I use a Vigenere and Autokey cipher encoder and decoder?

Encode and decode with a repeating Vigenère key or a self-extending Autokey stream, and watch the key stream build letter by letter. Enter the known values and inspect the result immediately. For example, Encode ATTACKATDAWN with keyword LEMON to get LXFOPVEFRNHR, then decode it back. Use the output to check your reasoning or shortlist a design, then verify the units, assumptions, and real-world limits that apply.

OutputCase, punctuation, digits, emoji and non-Latin text are preserved.

See the key stream repeat

Each letter of the message lines up with the keyword letter that shifts it, wrapping the keyword as many times as needed.

A repeating keyword is a weakness: once an attacker guesses the keyword length, each position becomes a plain shift cipher. Not secure encryption - do not use it to protect real secrets.
