Understand the system

Signed Doppler shift

A LEO satellite approaching a station raises received carrier frequency; recession lowers it. Use radial velocity along the line of sight, not total orbital speed. Here positive v_r means recession; the first-order approximation applies when speed is much smaller than c.

Δf≈−fcvr/c\Delta f\approx -f_c v_r/cΔf≈−fc​vr​/c

f_c: Hz; v_r: m/s, positive receding; c: m/s; Δf: Hz.

A faster carrier produces a larger Doppler shift for the same radial speed.

Source: finalExam.pdf · PDF page 1

Common mistakes

  • Convert km/s to m/s when c is m/s.
  • One slant range is not a full ground-to-ground relay path.
  • Do not assume orbital speed equals radial speed.
Formula reference and symbol definitions

First-order Doppler

Δf=−fcvr/c\Delta f=-f_cv_r/cΔf=−fc​vr​/c

f_c: Hz; v_r,c: m/s

v_r positive receding; |v_r| much smaller than c.

Example: At 2 GHz and recession velocity +6000 m/s, Δf = −2 × 10⁹ × 6000/(3 × 10⁸) = −40000 Hz.

Propagation delay

t=d/ct=d/ct=d/c

d: m; c: m/s; t: s

Geometric flight time only.

Example: A 36000 km path gives t = 36 × 10⁶/(3 × 10⁸) = 0.120 s one way.

Worked approach

Radial velocity

Flight time equals path length divided by propagation speed. A ground-to-ground satellite relay has uplink plus downlink path lengths; its round trip doubles that combined flight time. Processing, queuing and TDMA frame delays are additional. A metre-scale laboratory bench cannot demonstrate an orbital delay directly on an ordinary scope.

Questions engineers ask

Which topics does Doppler and Propagation Delay Tool cover?

Radial velocity; Approach and recession; One-way and round-trip delay.

How should I check my work in Doppler and Propagation Delay Tool?

Read the stated assumptions and source references, check units and independently repeat a worked example before relying on the result.

Does Doppler and Propagation Delay Tool operate real satellite equipment?

No. This is a browser-based educational resource. It does not transmit RF signals, command spacecraft or certify a working link.
