Understand the system

Pointing vocabulary

The subsatellite point is the surface intersection of the Earth-center-to-satellite line. Azimuth specifies a compass direction around the local horizon; elevation is the angle above that horizon. Together these are the look angles.

Source: finalExam.pdf · PDF page 3

Common mistakes

  • Longitude difference needs the correct sign and wrap.
  • GEO altitude is not every station’s slant range.
  • A numeric azimuth does not establish visibility below the horizon.

Worked approach

Subsatellite point

Form the vector from the station to the spacecraft and resolve it into local east, north and up components. Slant range is its length. atan2 uses both signs and preserves the azimuth quadrant; normalize the azimuth to 0–360°. Elevation at or below zero is not a usable unobstructed above-horizon path.
