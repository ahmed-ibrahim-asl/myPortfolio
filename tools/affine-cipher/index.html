How do I use a affine cipher encoder and decoder?

Encode and decode with a Caesar-style additive shift, a multiplicative cipher, or the combined affine cipher, with the modular-inverse math shown. Enter the known values and inspect the result immediately. For example, Encode AFFINECIPHER with a=5 and b=8 to get IHHWVCSWFRCP, then decode it back. Use the output to check your reasoning or shortlist a design, then verify the units, assumptions, and real-world limits that apply.

The formula behind Additive (Caesar)

C = (P + k) mod 26 - every letter shifts by the same fixed amount k.

This is a classical substitution cipher for learning modular arithmetic, not secure encryption. Do not use it to protect real secrets.
