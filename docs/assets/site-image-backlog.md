## Acceptance checklist for every future asset

- The concept is identifiable at card size.
- The focal object is not cropped at 16:9, 4:3, or narrow mobile card crops.
- Generated text is not used as interface copy.
- Gold marks identity; blue is limited to information or signal flow.
- No decorative Arabic watermark.
- No glowing brain, cyberpunk corridor, neon circuit wallpaper, or generic AI dashboard.
- Store the exact prompt and final public path next to the asset manifest.
