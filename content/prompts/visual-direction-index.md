Use these shortcuts as a shared vocabulary. A shortcut is the start of a brief, not the complete prompt. Always add the subject, audience, aspect ratio, required labels, and what must remain technically accurate.

## Explain structure

- `/blueprint`: orthographic engineering drawing with measured callouts and restrained line work.
- `/layer`: stacked layers that explain how a product or interface is assembled.
- `/explodeview`: separated components, aligned to their assembly order.
- `/machineview`: one clear technical perspective focused on functional parts.
- `/machineview360`: a consistent multi-angle rotation sheet.
- `/topview`: a strict overhead view for layouts, boards, and workspaces.

## Explain change

- `/sequence`: numbered frames showing a process in chronological order.
- `/beforeafter`: two matched frames with only the relevant change highlighted.
- `/defectview`: a diagnostic view that locates a fault, symptom, and likely cause.

## Present an idea

- `/handwritten`: annotated notebook treatment for teaching and early reasoning.
- `/3dbillboard`: bold dimensional headline or object on a simple campaign stage.
- `/floating3d`: isolated components arranged in depth for a cover image.
- `/magazinecover`: editorial hierarchy with a strong title and controlled secondary copy.
- `/metaad`: social advertisement composition with one promise and one action.
- `/adcreativecode`: product-led ad concept where code or output is the main evidence.
- `/goldenhour`: warm natural light for human or field-project photography.
- `/wideshot`: environmental context that shows where a system operates.
- `/footwaretechpac`: manufacturing specification sheet. Use only for apparel or footwear work.

## Portfolio cover recipe

Start with one shortcut, then add: the real tool name, the problem it solves, one visible input-to-output relationship, the ASL ink and gold palette, Archivo and Space Mono labels, flat lighting, no glossy sci-fi effects, no fake UI, and no unreadable text. Keep the image useful at both 16:9 and mobile card crops.
