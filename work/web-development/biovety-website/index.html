Website redesign and migration

From Eurokem to a clearer BioVety catalogue

Website redesign, catalogue structure, domain, hosting, and business email setup for a veterinary health business moving from its legacy Eurokem presence to the new BioVety identity.

I rebuilt the client's web presence around the BioVety identity and made its veterinary products easier to browse. The new website is complete, while the wider transition is still ongoing.

The work also covered the move behind the website

  4. Implemented the BioVety domain and hosting on GoDaddy because that was the client's chosen provider.
