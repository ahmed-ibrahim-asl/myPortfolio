# اللوش — experimental design brief

The user delegated applying all four design skills to a parallel copy and seeing what we can reach. Existing content and product behavior are settled. The design choice below is an experiment, not a production rebrand. No additional approval gate is needed to build the authorized preview.

Reading this as an engineering portfolio for people assessing tangible work, with an artifact-led language. The first viewport should show a real machine as well as who built it.

Modes: Experience for home/project discovery; Operate retained in engineering tools. Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 7, MOTION_INTENSITY 3, VISUAL_DENSITY 4.

Category rut: dark neon developer terminal. Predictable opposite: beige serif personal-brand essay. Neither fits the evidence.

Seven candidate worlds, ordered by resonance:
1. An engineering exhibition: deep blue gallery walls, large real project images, open exhibit captions, paths into case studies.
2. A laboratory sample archive: pale cool surfaces, oversized labels, image trays and a searchable artifact index.
3. A technical conference programme: saturated blue folios, sharply differentiated project and talk-like sections, direct navigation.
4. A service manual foldout: measured grey surfaces, annotated real hardware photography and clear linear reading order.
5. A model-making studio pinboard: blue and white mats, staggered project scale, portrait as maker attribution.
6. An underwater expedition briefing: navy and cyan, ROV evidence as opening, project log and instrument tools.
7. A signal-flow schematic: connected content regions grounded in hardware, firmware and interface work, no decorative fake measurements.

All use existing assets and actual links. No performance claims or invented data. Scope: replace homepage composition and align shared header/footer styling; retain tool interaction layouts and the rest of the site's routes.

## Resolved direction and composition

The concept seed assigned candidate 4: service-manual foldout, key `aff079b6`. The alternatives (racing garage, bolted futurist book, BBS terminal, cutting bench, neon circuit, transforming textile) were considered against audience identification and product clarity. None improves on a literal, readable view of the actual hardware without imposing a misleading mechanism or obscuring engineering content.

Composition A in `.impeccable/mocks/lush-compositions.png` carries forward under the user's delegated experimental implementation. Composition B delays the action, while C spends mobile space on a persistent rail. These were exploratory comps rather than approved final product screenshots. Actual existing assets replace placeholders; generated placeholder portrait and invented comp captions are not used.

| Ingredient | Implementation |

| Motion | Brief button feedback only; content visible from first paint; reduced-motion supported |

Palette: cool paper `#eef1f5`, ink `#17202e`, cobalt `#1748e8`, slate `#526070`, white. Dark mode uses navy paper `#101c30` and ice-blue accent `#a9c5ff`. No new factual claims. The mode serves a reader inspecting an actual build on a laptop or phone; the photo carries the mechanism.
